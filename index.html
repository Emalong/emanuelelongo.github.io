Emanuele Longo

Experimental physicist in spintronics, orbitronics and quantum materials

I develop experimental platforms to investigate spin, orbital and topological phenomena in quantum materials and thin-film heterostructures, with a focus on spin–charge conversion, orbital currents, magnetic resonance, magneto-optics and device-oriented functionalities.

Research

My research focuses on spintronics, orbitronics, topological quantum materials, magnetization dynamics, spin–charge conversion and advanced experimental methods including FMR, MOKE, XMCD and ARPES.

Selected topics

  • Spin and orbital transport in quantum materials
  • Topological materials and thin-film heterostructures
  • Magnetization dynamics and ferromagnetic resonance
  • Magneto-optical detection of spin accumulation
  • Device-oriented spintronic and orbitronic functionalities
